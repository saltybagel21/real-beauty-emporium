The salon

One salon for the whole family.

Real Beauty Emporium sits in York Street Boulevard, a few minutes from the middle of George. It started out as Real Earth Beauty & Hair Salon and grew into what it is now: stylists, a barber and colourist, nail technicians and beauty therapists working side by side, so you can get your roots done while your son gets a clipper cut and your mum has her MediHeel pedicure.

Appointments are booked through Fresha and confirmed instantly. You pay at the salon, cash or card. Walk-ins are welcome when there is a gap, but Saturdays fill up, so book ahead.

Our team is more than just professionals. We are family, dedicated to bringing out your best. From expert stylists to skilled beauty therapists and everything in between, each of us is here to make your experience unforgettable. Come visit us and let our hands work wonders.
